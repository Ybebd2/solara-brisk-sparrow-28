An open-world crime story built for the **gta6-01** bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) — a sun-soaked island city where Kai Mercer works 8 jobs across 7 districts, outruns the PCPD, and hunts 20 Golden Geckos.

Referral: [make.fun/r/mpd7ezsn](https://make.fun/r/mpd7ezsn)

## The game

- **Open world** — 7 districts (Marina Bay, Old Port, Downtown, Neon Row, Palm Heights, Dockside, Solara Beach), a turquoise ocean with sailboats, cream-sand beach with umbrellas, palm-lined avenues, multi-story buildings with individual windows, billboards, traffic lights, and street props.
- **8 story jobs** — type-based completion: walk to a contact, drive a package across town, hold a spot to lift a ledger, jack a fresh ride, lose the heat, and pull the Solara Job (hold the bank door, then drive the getaway to the Marina).
- **Wanted system** — 5 gold stars, a PCPD RESPONSE panel with named units and live distances (KITE-1, MARLIN-3, CORAL-48…), HIDDEN / IN SIGHT badges with an evading meter, cruisers, officers, roadblocks at 3+ stars, and a police helicopter whose searchlight beam is computed live from the lamp under its nose to the bright pool on the ground.
- **Crew** — Mara (GHOST, −1 star), Sully (PIT CREW, full repair), Kiki (LOOKOUT, patrols revealed).
- **Driving & shooting** — carjack any traffic car, 5 weapons (VESPER pistol, JACKAL SMG, LONGSHOT rifle, RIOT shotgun, fists), vehicle HUD with condition, speed, gear and radio.
- **Radio** — PUERTO 88.3, CELESTE 98.1, SOLARA 98.3, NEON 101.5.
- **Career** — ranks ROOKIE → RUNNER → HUSTLER → FIXER, pause menu with map, story, career stats, crew, settings, controls, save/load.

## Controls

WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · mouse drag camera · F enter/exit car · E start job · click shoot · R reload · 1–5 weapons · Q radio · M map · F9 autopilot · Z/X/C crew · Esc pause.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no dependencies — plain ES modules + vendored Three.js.
